The earlier roadmap proposed 12 lessons. This plan expands it to 18 so learners can start independently and exceptions, accessibility, dates and editorial review receive explicit teaching. Review the [concept graph, assessments and every edge reason](concept-graph.md), the [48 provision coverage targets](coverage.md) and the [source register and reading limits](../source-register.md). `requires` means genuine prerequisite knowledge; `suggests` adds helpful context without blocking progress. Lesson prerequisites are derived from required concept edges. Each concept is taught once within this course, with one to three concepts per lesson and one case exercise at the end of each module.

The running fictional organisation, **Harbour Services**, operates a customer agent, integrates an external generation service and publishes a campaign. Cases change one legally relevant fact at a time. T06 tests interaction and accessibility; T12 follows outputs through generation, editing and export; T18 concerns a prerecorded avatar, biometric exposure and a public-interest report. T18 does not assess agent-specific interaction knowledge or criminal-law authorisation exceptions absent from its prerequisite ancestry.

The teaching will distinguish binding law, Commission interpretation and voluntary implementation methods. In particular:

- Provider marking and deployer disclosure can both apply to the same output.
- Agent principal and supervisory disclosures come from the Commission's interpretation of Article 50(1).
- Upstream marking can support a downstream system provider's compliance, but that provider must verify the route actually works in its product.
- Article 111(4)'s transition concerns only Article 50(2) for qualifying existing systems. The Code's interoperability milestone is a separate implementation commitment.
- Creative works receive adapted disclosure; artistic purpose does not remove disclosure altogether.
- The text exception needs **(human review OR editorial control) AND editorial responsibility**. Substantive AI changes after review require reconsideration.
- Code adherence supports demonstrating compliance; it is not conclusive proof. Its optional measures are identified explicitly. A 200-token technical definition is not a statutory exemption.
- Disclosure does not legalise prohibited biometric use or settle data protection, intellectual property, consumer protection or sector-specific law.

The legal baseline is the 27 July 2026 consolidation, checked against the Official Journal amendment. The English Transparency Guidelines and both sections of the final Code have been read; the retained Commission and Board adequacy assessments have selected reading limits recorded honestly. Implementation examples will teach testing and procurement questions, not claim certification of any model, platform, watermark or standard.
