# Conformity and market access — lesson outline

**English · 30 lessons · 5 modules · 64 concepts. Approved; authored lessons remain in testing.**

Self-contained foundations are retaught. Cases compare fictional HR and medical-product releases, with biometric, critical-infrastructure and public-deployer variations. Each module ends with a practical decision.

This is the approved syllabus for the editable teaching package. See [review scope and cases](README.md), [every concept and prerequisite reason](concept-graph.md), [provision coverage](coverage.md) and [reading limits](source-checks.md).

## F0 — Define the release and assessment boundary

| ID  | Lesson                                            | Concepts taught here                                               | Requires lessons   |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------ |
| F01 | What exactly are we releasing?                    | Market access map; Assessed system identity; Provider release duty | None               |
| F02 | Separate permission from high-risk classification | High risk route; Prohibited use precheck                           | F01                |
| F03 | Find the applicable date                          | Provision specific timeline; Existing system transition            | F02                |
| F04 | Follow the product-law route                      | Sector assessment boundary; Dual route priority                    | F02                |
| F05 | Turn requirements into an evidence dossier        | Requirement evidence matrix; Technical file index                  | F01, F02           |
| F06 | Case: record the release boundary                 | Release scope case                                                 | F02, F03, F04, F05 |

**F01 decision:** Identify the distinct roles of assessment, declaration, marking and registration. Specify intended purpose, version, boundaries, dependencies and release form for one system. Identify the provider accountable for the proposed market release or own-use putting into service.

**F02 decision:** Separate Annex III, Annex I Section A, Annex I Section B and a documented non-high-risk conclusion. Reject the inference that an assessment or a certificate can authorise an Article 5 prohibition.

**F03 decision:** Distinguish Section 4 and Section 5 application from the later Section 1–3 dates; flag a case needing temporal interpretation. Apply the existing-system transition conditions to dated case facts without inventing universal grandfathering.

**F04 decision:** Choose the relevant sector procedure and identify its AI requirements, conditional third-party option and transitional assessor evidence. Select the sector procedure where the same system falls under both Annex I Section A and Annex III.

**F05 decision:** Map an applicable requirement to evidence, an owner, a gap and a release decision. Locate a missing system-specific record in the technical-file index; distinguish an index from proof of conformity.

**F06 decision:** Produce a dated scope record for fictional HR and medical-product releases with explicit unknowns and sources.

## F1 — Choose and document conformity evidence

| ID  | Lesson                                       | Concepts taught here                                                         | Requires lessons |
| --- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| F07 | Check what a standard actually proves        | Official Journal citation check; Limited presumption                         | None             |
| F08 | Map covered requirements and remaining gaps  | Standard coverage matrix; Alternative conformity evidence                    | F05, F07         |
| F09 | Use common specifications correctly          | Common specification trigger; Equivalent technical solution                  | F08              |
| F10 | Limit data and cybersecurity presumptions    | Contextual data presumption; Cybersecurity presumption; CRA interplay lookup | F07              |
| F11 | Keep standards and supplier claims current   | Standards update watch; International certificate boundary                   | F07              |
| F12 | Case: reject an unsupported compliance claim | Conformity evidence case                                                     | F08, F09, F10    |

**F07 decision:** Verify the exact standard, version, Official Journal citation, restrictions and relevant scope. Limit a presumption of conformity to the requirements actually covered by its legal basis.

**F08 decision:** Compare cited standard coverage with each applicable requirement and mark uncovered requirements. Specify evidence for a requirement outside a presumption without treating voluntary standards as the only permissible solution.

**F09 decision:** Distinguish a formally adopted common specification from a draft, guidance or an industry document. Identify the justification and equivalent technical evidence needed when not complying with a common specification.

**F10 decision:** Identify the limited Article 10(4) presumption for data reflecting the intended geographical, contextual, behavioural or functional setting. Check whether an Official Journal-referenced cybersecurity scheme covers the relevant Article 15 requirements. Locate the CRA scope, deemed-compliance conditions and assessment interplay before accepting a cybersecurity compliance claim.

**F11 decision:** Record version, coverage, citation and procedure changes that require a fresh release review. Explain why an ISO management-system certificate or a supplier assertion alone does not establish AI Act conformity.

**F12 decision:** Find the coverage gap in a fictional certification pitch and request the evidence needed to resolve it.

## F2 — Verify the assessor and its scope

| ID  | Lesson                                       | Concepts taught here                                                            | Requires lessons   |
| --- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| F13 | Who may assess this system?                  | Notifying authority; Notified body; Notified scope verification                 | None               |
| F14 | How designation becomes effective            | Designation evidence; Notification objection window; Unified sector designation | F04, F13           |
| F15 | Check independence and competence            | Assessor independence; Assessor confidentiality; Assessor competence            | F13                |
| F16 | Control subcontracting and proportionality   | Subcontracting accountability; Proportionate assessment                         | F05, F15           |
| F17 | Respond when designation changes             | Designation change response; Competence challenge; Assessor coordination        | F13                |
| F18 | Case: select and retain a qualified assessor | Assessor selection case                                                         | F13, F15, F16, F17 |

**F13 decision:** Distinguish the authority designating and monitoring assessors from the assessor reviewing the product. Distinguish a designated conformity assessor from a consultant or ordinary certification provider. Check the body identification, notified task and system codes, status and any third-country agreement basis.

**F14 decision:** Distinguish accreditation, other designation evidence and the Article 32 presumption for a body from product compliance. Distinguish the two-week and two-month objection windows and their effect on notification. Recognise when a sector law permits unified designation and what evidence is needed for transitional assessment powers.

**F15 decision:** Identify an assessor conflict or resource/process failure that undermines the required independent assessment. Distinguish confidentiality protection from refusal to provide lawfully required assessment evidence. Match the assessment scope to staff competence, methods, records and conformity-assessment experience.

**F16 decision:** Identify the consent, qualification, record and responsibility conditions for an assessor subcontractor. Distinguish proportionate assessment effort from lowering protection or omitting an applicable requirement.

**F17 decision:** Identify the certificate, file-transfer and continuity checks triggered by designation restriction, withdrawal or cessation. Locate the Commission challenge and corrective procedure when assessor competence is disputed. Identify the purpose of information exchange and coordination without treating it as a new product authorisation.

**F18 decision:** Select or reject a fictional assessor using scope, independence, subcontracting and continuity evidence.

## F3 — Carry out and maintain the assessment

| ID  | Lesson                                       | Concepts taught here                                               | Requires lessons   |
| --- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------ |
| F19 | Perform internal control                     | Internal control assessment; Design file consistency               | F02, F05           |
| F20 | Choose the biometric assessment procedure    | Biometric assessment route; Special authority assessor             | F02, F08           |
| F21 | Prepare for a notified-body assessment       | QMS approval; Technical documentation assessment; QMS surveillance | F05, F13           |
| F22 | Manage certificates and assessment decisions | Certificate lifecycle; Assessment result sharing                   | F15, F21           |
| F23 | Reassess changes to the system               | Substantial modification test; Predetermined learning change       | F01, F05           |
| F24 | Case: choose and defend the assessment route | Assessment route case                                              | F19, F20, F21, F23 |

**F19 decision:** Specify the provider checks for Annex III points 2–8 without adding an AI Act notified-body requirement. Check whether design, development and post-market monitoring agree with the assessed technical documentation.

**F20 decision:** Distinguish the Annex VI/Annex VII choice from mandatory Annex VII when standards or common-specification conditions fail. Identify when the specified market surveillance authority acts as the notified body for the assessment.

**F21 decision:** Prepare the quality-management application and route a proposed QMS change for the required decision. Identify the application, necessary data/testing evidence, restricted model-access conditions and refusal/retraining consequences. Identify the ongoing premises access, information and periodic-audit duties after QMS approval.

**F22 decision:** Check validity, reassessment, supplement dependency, corrective action, restriction, suspension, withdrawal and appeal. Distinguish notifications to authorities from exchanges with other bodies and preserve confidentiality.

**F23 decision:** Evaluate whether a change requires a new assessment, including continued use by the same deployer and changed provider duties. Distinguish initially documented predetermined learning changes from an unrestricted exemption for later model updates.

**F24 decision:** Defend the HR, biometric and stipulated medical-product procedures and resolve a proposed post-assessment change.

## F4 — Complete and defend the release record

| ID  | Lesson                                               | Concepts taught here                                                                     | Requires lessons   |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| F25 | Recognise a genuine exceptional authorisation        | Conformity derogation authorisation; Emergency use boundary; Sector derogation boundary  | F02, F04           |
| F26 | Prepare the EU declaration                           | Provider declaration responsibility; Declaration content check                           | F01, F05           |
| F27 | Apply the correct CE marking                         | CE marking accessibility; CE notified body number                                        | F13, F26           |
| F28 | Register the right actor and system                  | Registration role test; High risk registration record; Exempt system registration record | F01, F02, F26      |
| F29 | Choose the public, restricted or national record     | Registration destination test; Public deployer registration                              | F28                |
| F30 | Case: decide whether the release dossier is complete | Market release case                                                                      | F24, F26, F27, F29 |

**F25 decision:** Identify a justified, authority-granted, territorially and temporally limited derogation while Section 2 compliance remains required. Identify the narrowly specified urgent law-enforcement/civil-protection conditions, immediate request and refusal consequences. Use the relevant product-law derogation for an Annex I Section A product instead of treating Article 46 as a general shortcut.

**F26 decision:** Identify provider responsibility, machine-readable signed form, authority language, ten-year retention, updates and combined-law declaration. Check the eight declaration fields and evidence for any applicable personal-data statement; a signature does not prove compliance.

**F27 decision:** Select digital access and visible, legible, indelible marking or the specified fallback, including other applicable CE laws. Identify when the body identification number accompanies marking and promotional mentions, without inventing a body for internal control.

**F28 decision:** Decide who registers before market/service, including a non-high-risk Article 6(3) conclusion; registration does not confer approval. Prepare the provider fields, conditional certificate attachments, declaration and instructions with their specified exceptions. Prepare the non-high-risk record using the remaining fields and recognise that points 7 and 9 were deleted.

**F29 decision:** Choose the EU public, secured non-public or national registration destination for the stated system and context. Identify the specified public/Union/acting-on-behalf deployer duty and its fields without extending it to every private deployer.

**F30 decision:** Make a supported ready/not-ready/needs-specialist-review decision with a dated route record, remaining evidence gaps and change controls.
