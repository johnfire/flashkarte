# Source reading and unresolved lookups

Checked on **7 October 2026**. This is a record of the source work for the proposed syllabus, not a declaration that any commercial product is compliant.

## Reading completed

The retained [English consolidation](../sources/ai-act-2026-07-27-en.pdf) supplies the working legal text. Articles 28–49 and Annexes IV–VIII and XIV were read in full. Targeted bridges were reread for Article 2(2), Article 6(1)–(5), Article 3 definitions (12) and (19)–(25), Articles 8–18 and 25, the Article 71 database, Article 111(2) and Article 113. The source register records the file hash. A direct web opening of the consolidation timed out; the retained PDF was used rather than claiming that retrieval succeeded.

The Commission's [framework overview](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai) was checked for the amendment identity and phased application. The [standardisation page](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-act-standardisation) and [standardisation FAQ](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/understanding-standardisation-ai-act) were read for the citation process, limited presumptions and the boundary of international management-system certification. These pages explain the framework; the operative statute controls lesson claims.

The Commission's [notified-bodies page](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/goods/building-blocks/notified-bodies_en) was read for assessor competence, independence and the NANDO identification/task lookup. The [Service Desk display of Article 49](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-49) was compared with the retained text.

The official [Cyber Resilience Act PDF](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:L_202402847) was used after HTML retrieval returned a verification page. Its Article 2, definitions 3(1)–(2), and Article 12 were read to ground the limited cybersecurity bridge. The syllabus will teach a source lookup for scope, conditions and assessment interplay, rather than complete CRA instruction. Actual cases need a current-version and application-date check.

Article 30 of [Regulation 765/2008](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02008R0765-20210716) was read in its original and consolidated forms to check general CE principles. Annex II text was also checked. No marking artwork or actual product interface was audited.
