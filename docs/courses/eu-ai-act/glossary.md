## Translation review checklist

For each lesson record the English source revision, DE/CS legal locators, reviewer and status. Check terminology in running sentences, including Czech case endings and natural German prose. Re-run concept/assessment coverage after translation; do not introduce new concepts or change prerequisite meaning silently. A new English legal correction must identify every affected translated screen and answer explanation.

Intimate-media consent in Article 5(1)(ba) has its own wording. Do not substitute Article 3(59)'s definition for real-world-testing consent merely because both concern consent. Terms from incorporated sector or criminal-law instruments need their own glossary entries after those sources are read.
