Direct EUR-Lex requests for supplementary GDPR/directive texts encountered access challenges. Those failed retrievals are not evidence of reading the provisions. H28 therefore teaches the separate lookup and uses the EDPB overview for the general distinction. Source the actual trigger before adding specialist data-law examples.

No current availability claim is made for simplified documentation forms, the FRIA template, harmonised standards, model contract terms or common specifications. H13/H28 identify their conditional role and the need to verify availability. National biometric authorisation and worker-information procedures remain jurisdiction-specific lookups. The Northbridge cases use stipulated fictional facts, not evidence about an actual supplier.

The detailed serious-incident timetable and conformity-assessment procedures are explicit later-course handoffs. Recheck law, guidance, forms and national implementation when revising or applying the course to a real product.
