# Legal baseline and remaining reading

Working version: **CELEX 02024R1689-20260727**, consolidated on 27 July 2026. The official English, German and Czech consolidated PDFs and the three language versions of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 are retained in `sources/`. The original English Official Journal Act is also retained. See the [source register](source-register.md) for URLs, hashes and actual reading limits.

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 was published on 12 July 2024 and entered into force on 1 August 2024, twenty days after publication. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 was published on 24 July 2026 and entered into force on 27 July 2026. The authentic Official Journal instruments govern; consolidation is a convenient working reference with a documentation-only notice.

## First-release changes that must survive simplification

| Provision                                  | Required teaching distinction                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Article 4                                  | Use the amended duty to support staff and other relevant persons' AI literacy, taking knowledge, experience, training and context into account. Do not substitute the old wording or invent a mandatory certificate.                                                                        |
| Article 6(1)                               | Product coverage and mandatory third-party conformity assessment are cumulative conditions; AI can be a safety component or itself a covered product. Independent placement on the market does not remove the test.                                                                         |
| Article 6(1a)–(1c)                         | Separate exclusively non-safety functions, the failure/malfunction qualification, and third-party assessment required solely for risks other than health and safety. Do not reduce the rule to “anything in a regulated product is high-risk.”                                              |
| Annex I                                    | Section A point 1 is deleted; machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 appears at Section B point 21. Article 2(2) limits which AI Act provisions apply to Section B sectors; classification alone is not the whole obligations analysis.                                                        |
| Article 6(3)                               | A listed Annex III use case needs the no-significant-risk gateway and at least one listed condition to qualify for the derogation. Profiling of natural persons overrides this derogation within Annex III; profiling is not a universal rule for every AI system.                          |
| Article 6(4), Article 49                   | Provider documentation, registration before placement/service and production of the assessment on request are separate tasks. Restricted registration sections and Annex VIII fields must be checked.                                                                                       |
| Annex VIII Section B                       | Deleted points 7 and 9 must not be requested as if still operative registration fields. The separate obligation to document an assessment remains.                                                                                                                                          |
| Article 5(1)(ba), (bb)                     | Intimate-media and child-abuse material/performance provisions are additions to the original prohibitions. They need their own elements, cross-references and application date.                                                                                                             |
| Article 5(1a), (1b)                        | Separate provider placement/service conditions, foreseeable and reproducible misuse without significant technical modification, inadequate safeguards and deployer purpose. Do not treat every possible misuse as the same statutory trigger. Teach the specific manipulation boundary too. |
| Article 5(1)(f), Article 3(39), recital 18 | Emotion/intent inference depends on biometric data. Physical fatigue is not automatically an emotion: recital 18 excludes physical states such as pain and fatigue from this definition. Other risks and obligations can still apply.                                                       |
| Article 5(1)(h), (2)–(7)                   | Remote identification exceptions require exact objectives, necessity/proportionality, safeguards, impact assessment, registration and authorisation. Urgency is a limited procedure, not blanket permission. National authorisation, notification and reporting are separately identified.  |
| Article 5(3)                               | Refused urgent authorisation requires immediate cessation and deletion; an adverse legal decision cannot be made solely on the real-time identification output.                                                                                                                             |

## Application and transition register

These are provision-specific starting points, not a single “AI Act deadline”. Article 113 supplies application dates; Article 111 adds transitional treatment. Cases must specify relevant placement/service dates and facts.

| Date             | Provision or event                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 August 2024    | Original Act enters into force.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 2 February 2025  | Chapters I and II apply, subject to the separate dates of the newly inserted Article 5 provisions.                                                                                                |
| 2 August 2025    | Chapter III Section 4, Chapter V, Chapter VII, Chapter XII and Article 78 apply; Article 101 is excepted from this early date.                                                                    |
| 27 July 2026     | Amending regulation enters into force; Article 113(d) gives this date to Articles 102–110.                                                                                                        |
| 2 August 2026    | Default application date, including Article 50, subject to specific exceptions and transitions.                                                                                                   |
| 2 December 2026  | New Article 5(1)(ba), (bb), (1a), (1b) apply. Article 111(4) gives providers of synthetic-content-generating systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 until this date for Article 50(2). |
| 2 August 2027    | Article 111(3): compliance date for GPAI models placed on the market before 2 August 2025.                                                                                                        |
| 2 December 2027  | Chapter III Sections 1–3 for Annex III systems, except Article 6(5). Article 6(5) has its own guidance deadline; it is not delayed by this entry.                                                 |
| 2 August 2028    | Chapter III Sections 1–3 for Annex I systems, except Article 6(5).                                                                                                                                |
| 2 August 2030    | Article 111(2) public-authority high-risk system transition; read the complete conditions when applying it.                                                                                       |
| 31 December 2030 | Article 111(1) transition for specified large-scale IT systems placed before 2 August 2027.                                                                                                       |

Article 111(2) also conditions application to certain existing high-risk systems on significant design changes after the relevant application date. Do not teach a universal grandfathering rule. Other transitions and technical real-world-testing provisions receive full treatment in later blocks.

## Official-language comparison

Article 6 and its inserted paragraphs were read in EN/DE/CS. Article 5 additions, their distinct date and selected definitions were compared; this is a targeted comparison, not a complete three-language audit. Before every translated lesson, check all its cited provisions and options against the official language text.

The German corrigendum, OJ 2025/90802 of 9 October 2025, corrects recital 177, Article 3(40)'s ancillary-function exclusion and the former Article 111(2) wording. The Czech corrigendum, OJ 2026/90258 of 27 March 2026, corrects provider/deployer grammar, Article 19(1)'s provider designation, the Article 50 heading and Article 57(5) punctuation. The 2026 amendment replaces Article 111(2), so its former wording is not the current rule.
