# Personal-data incident response

1. Contain the incident and preserve relevant logs without altering evidence.
2. Record scope, affected systems, data categories, affected people, and time.
3. Assess risk to individuals and decide whether supervisory-authority notice
   is required within 72 hours.
4. Notify affected people without undue delay when the risk is high.
5. Remediate the cause, document the decision and outcome, and run a
   post-incident review.

Keep this record restricted to authorised operators and include the decision
when notification is not required.
